**Hyrule Warriors AOI Recomp Optimized 1.0.3** adds two-player co-op and a reworked controls menu.

## What's new

- **Two-player co-op.** The game's two-player mode now works. Each player is their own Switch controller, fed only by their own device, so one player's input never reaches the other.
  - **Mix and match devices:** Player 1 on a gamepad and Player 2 on keyboard and mouse, or two gamepads of any brand (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro, generic), even different brands.
  - **Separate rumble and motion:** each follows that player's own gamepad.
  - **Hot-plugging:** gamepads plugged in or out while playing are picked up.
- **Players and console settings** at the top of **Esc → Controls**:
  - **Player 1 / Player 2 input:** Auto, Keyboard and mouse, or a connected gamepad by name.
  - **Player 1 / Player 2 controller:** Pro Controller or Joy-Con pair.
  - **Console mode:** Docked or Handheld, switchable live. The game is told the console was docked or undocked, like real hardware.
- **Per-player button bindings.** A **Bindings for: Player 1 / Player 2** row above the remapping list switches whose keys and gamepad buttons you're editing.
  - Each player's bindings only affect that player.
  - Player 1 keeps your existing bindings.
- **Screen settings.**
  - **Fullscreen** is now a Windowed / Fullscreen setting that switches live, not only with F11.
  - **Window size** can be changed from the menu again.
  - **Windowed mode** starts at 1280×720 instead of filling the screen.

## Updating from 1.0.2

Extract 1.0.3, open **Hyrule Warriors AOI Recomp Optimized Compiler.exe**, choose the same NUP and output folder, and click **Compile / update**. Saves and settings are kept.

## Using it

Download the ZIP below, extract all of it, open **Hyrule Warriors AOI Recomp Optimized Compiler.exe**, choose your own game NUP and an output folder, then click **Compile / update**. Nothing else needs installing.

> Bring your own matching game files. This ZIP contains no game, keys, firmware or artwork.

## Known limits

- Some battles don't allow two players; the game decides where co-op is available.
- Split-screen play in every mission has not been verified yet.
- 90 and 120 FPS speed up the whole game (1.5× / 2×). For smooth motion at normal speed, play at 60 FPS with frame generation such as Lossless Scaling LSFG 2×.

## Requirements

- Windows 10/11 x64, an AVX2 CPU and a Vulkan GPU (32 GiB RAM recommended)
- At least 20 GiB of free space for the output
- Hyrule Warriors: Age of Imprisonment, title 04003F40232DE000, version 1.0.3 (NCM 196608), as a NUP
